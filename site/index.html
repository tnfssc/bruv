Ask once. Three agents race.

/race gives one task to several agents, each in its own copy of your repo, starting from your current changes. They run your checks. You get a scoreboard, press Enter, and the winner lands in your working tree. The rest are cleaned up.

  • Starts from where you are. Uncommitted and untracked files included. Your files aren't touched.
  • Proof, not promises. Each agent reports whether your checks pass. bruv runs them again after you pick.
  • Nothing left behind. Worktrees and branches are removed after you choose.

Install

  1. Install Pi
    curl -fsSL https://pi.dev/install.sh | sh
  2. Add bruv
    pi install git:github.com/tnfssc/bruv
  3. Start pi, run /login with your ChatGPT account, then /bruv-setup.
