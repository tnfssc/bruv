b488c57f3f9f1688e31c53daee99e29dd1d0baa2; a product-only edit does not change
upstream identity. Apply the old canonical patch to a disposable checkout at that
revision, edit actual source and filenames, then run:

    bun integrations/t3/build/regenerate-patch.ts /absolute/path/to/patched-checkout

IDs and filenames), and calls verifyWebSource before publishing the patch. The real

verified payload manifests are generated by the build pipeline, not manually edited.

complete payload receipt/build can pass. Do not leave a disposable source checkout
